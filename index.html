  • Apple Music or Spotify

    Scan the QR code, search, add. No app or music account needed. Reorders reflect instantly.

One party, multiple zones

One device manages a zone. Other devices can join it.

  1. Main Zone

    An iPad, Apple TV, or Mac manages the queue for a zone. Use multiple devices to set up multiple audio zones (upstairs, kitchen, etc).

  2. Displays

    Any other iPad, Apple TV, or Mac can join a zone as a remote display. Pair an iPad with an Apple TV hub to let guests queue without a phone.

  3. Phones

    Guests scan any QR code on a hub or a display and add songs with their own phone, no app or subscription needed.

Fast. Private. Beautiful.

Canopy Music was designed to be incredibly performant and incredibly private.

  1. Blazing fast

    A local server running on each hub means queue updates reflect instantly on all devices.

  2. Private

    No accounts, analytics, or tracking. Network requests are made only to Apple Music, Spotify, and Sonos.

  3. Designed with intent

    Every aspect of Canopy Music was human-designed to be gorgeous and intuitive. Built with AI assistance.
