A party jukebox. Pick a playlist, put it on a big screen, and let your guests queue songs from their phones.

Get it on GitHub

Build it with Xcode. Free for noncommercial use.

  • Guests queue from their phones

    Scan the QR code, search, add. No app or music account needed. Anyone can drag the list to reorder.

  • Hosts on iPad, Mac or Apple TV

    The big screen shows what’s playing, what’s next, and a QR code for guests to join.

  • Apple Music and Spotify

    Plays from Apple Music, plus Spotify on iPad. Requests and your playlist share one list.

Canopy Music has no accounts or tracking.
